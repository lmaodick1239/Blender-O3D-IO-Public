### Mathematical boundary

For a nonsingular affine linear transform `A`, an outward normal follows `normalize(A^-T n)`; translation must not enter a direction transform, and triangle cross-product orientation depends on `det(A)`. These are **M** claims, not assertions about OMSI's chosen handedness, normal sign or cull state. The reference [normal expression](../../.gitignorefolder/Blender-OMSI-Exporter/io_export_o3d/exporter.py#L248-L251) uses a 4×4 matrix with a 3D vector; it is not authority for copying translated-normal behavior. Reference local Euler/location/scale construction also does not justify restricting this add-on's parent/quaternion/world-matrix support.
